## CoinGecko Crypto Prices [#price]

`coingecko/price` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Crypto & DeFi, Finance & Markets

**Live crypto prices by ticker or name: price, 24h and 7d change, market cap, volume, 24h range, ATH.**

Current market data for up to 10 coins named by ticker ('btc'), CoinGecko id ('ethereum') or name ('Shiba Inu'), quoted in any major fiat or crypto currency: price, 24-hour and 7-day change, market cap and rank, 24h volume, high and low, and the all-time high with the distance from it. Prices refresh about every minute. For the leaderboard use coingecko/top; for what people are searching use coingecko/trending; for DeFi protocol value locked use defillama/protocol.

Related: [`coingecko/top`](/docs/tools/coingecko#top), [`coingecko/trending`](/docs/tools/coingecko#trending), [`defillama/protocol`](/docs/tools/defillama#protocol).

### Input [#price-input]

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `coins` | array of string | yes | Up to 10 coins by ticker, CoinGecko id or name: \['btc', 'ethereum', 'Shiba Inu'\]. A ticker shared by several coins means the largest by market cap. 1–10 items. |
| `vs_currency` | string | no | Quote currency, lower case: usd, eur, ngn, jpy, btc, eth… Pattern `^[a-z]{3,5}$`. Default `"usd"`. |

## CoinGecko Top Coins [#top]

`coingecko/top` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Crypto & DeFi, Finance & Markets

**The largest coins by market cap, overall or within a category (layer-1, meme, stablecoins, AI…).**

The crypto market-cap leaderboard: up to 50 coins ranked by market cap with price, 24h and 7d change, volume and 24h range, optionally within one CoinGecko category such as 'layer-1', 'meme-token', 'stablecoins' or 'artificial-intelligence'. Use it for 'what are the biggest coins' or a sector overview; for specific coins use coingecko/price.

Related: [`coingecko/price`](/docs/tools/coingecko#price), [`coingecko/trending`](/docs/tools/coingecko#trending).

### Input [#top-input]

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `limit` | integer | no | From 1 to 50. Default `10`. |
| `vs_currency` | string | no | Pattern `^[a-z]{3,5}$`. Default `"usd"`. |
| `category` | string | no | A CoinGecko category id: 'layer-1', 'meme-token', 'stablecoins', 'decentralized-finance-defi', 'artificial-intelligence'. At most 60 characters. |

## CoinGecko Trending Coins [#trending]

`coingecko/trending` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Crypto & DeFi

**The coins most searched on CoinGecko in the last 24 hours, with price and 24h change.**

CoinGecko's trending list: the coins most searched by its users in the last 24 hours (about 15), top first, with market-cap rank, USD price, 24-hour change and market cap. A sentiment signal, not a ranking by size (that is coingecko/top).
