## Open-Meteo 16-Day Forecast [#forecast]

`open-meteo/forecast` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Weather & Earth

**Weather now plus a daily forecast up to 16 days ahead for any place: highs, lows, rain chance, wind, UV.**

Current conditions and a day-by-day forecast (1-16 days) from Open-Meteo's blend of national weather models, for a place name or coordinates. Each day has the condition, min/max temperature, total precipitation, the highest chance of precipitation, max wind, max UV index, sunrise and sunset in local time. Use it for trips and plans beyond a few days; for hourly detail over the next 5 days use openweather/forecast, and for past days use open-meteo/history.

Related: [`open-meteo/history`](/docs/tools/open-meteo#history), [`openweather/forecast`](/docs/tools/openweather#forecast), [`openweather/current`](/docs/tools/openweather#current).

| Field | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `place` | string | no | A city or place name: 'Lagos', 'Lake Tahoe', 'Bondi Beach'. 2–100 characters. |
| `country_code` | string | no | ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 code to pick the right place: 'NG', 'US'. 2–2 characters. |
| `lat` | number | no | Latitude, with lon (instead of place). From -90 to 90. |
| `lon` | number | no | Longitude, with lat. From -180 to 180. |
| `days` | integer | no | Days ahead, 1-16. From 1 to 16. Default `7`. |
| `units` | string | no | metric (°C, m/s, mm) or imperial (°F, mph, inch). One of `metric`, `imperial`. Default `"metric"`. |

## Open-Meteo Weather History [#history]

`open-meteo/history` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Weather & Earth

**What the weather was on any day since 1940, anywhere: highs, lows, mean, rain and wind, day by day.**

Daily observed-equivalent weather for a past date or a range of up to 31 days, from 1940 to yesterday: condition, min/max/mean temperature, precipitation total, max wind, sunrise and sunset. Older dates come from the ERA5 reanalysis (about 25 km grid); the last 92 days come from archived high-resolution forecasts, so very recent days are available too. Use it to check claims about past weather, plan by climate, or compare years. Future dates belong to open-meteo/forecast.

Related: [`open-meteo/forecast`](/docs/tools/open-meteo#forecast).

## Open-Meteo Marine Forecast [#marine]

`open-meteo/marine` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Weather & Earth

**Wave height, period and direction plus swell and sea temperature, now and for up to 8 days.**

Sea state for a coastal place or ocean coordinates: significant wave height, wave direction and period, swell height and period and sea-surface temperature now, then daily maxima for up to 8 days. Good for surf, sailing, ferries and beach plans. A place name is geocoded to its town centre and the nearest sea grid cell is used; an inland point has no sea data and answers not found, so pass coordinates just offshore if needed. For weather on land use open-meteo/forecast.

Related: [`open-meteo/forecast`](/docs/tools/open-meteo#forecast).
