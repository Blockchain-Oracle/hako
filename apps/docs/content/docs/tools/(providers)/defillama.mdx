## DefiLlama Top Chains by TVL [#chains]

`defillama/chains` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Crypto & DeFi, Finance & Markets

**Blockchains ranked by DeFi total value locked, with each one's share of all TVL.**

Ranks every chain DefiLlama tracks by current DeFi TVL (USD) and returns the top N with its share of the total, gas-token symbol and EVM chain id where it has one. Current snapshot only. For one protocol use defillama/protocol.

Related: [`defillama/protocol`](/docs/tools/defillama#protocol).

## DefiLlama Protocol TVL [#protocol]

`defillama/protocol` · **$0.001** per call (local) · Crypto & DeFi, Finance & Markets

**A DeFi protocol's current TVL, 1h/1d/7d change, category, chains and its biggest chains by TVL.**

Looks up a DeFi protocol by DefiLlama slug or name and returns its total value locked in USD, the 1-hour, 1-day and 7-day % change, category, token symbol, market cap, website, the chains it runs on and its top 8 chains by TVL. A family slug such as 'aave' or 'uniswap' sums its versions (listed under children). Current values only, no history charts; borrowed and staked amounts are excluded from TVL as DefiLlama does by default. For chain totals use defillama/chains.

Related: [`defillama/chains`](/docs/tools/defillama#chains), [`serper/news`](/docs/tools/serper#news), [`hako/answer`](/docs/tools/hako#answer).
